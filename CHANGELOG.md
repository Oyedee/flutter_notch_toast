## 0.1.0

* Initial release: a Flutter port of the
  [notch-toast](https://github.com/Devsethi3/notch-toast) React package.
* `toast` API with default, success, error, warning, info, loading and
  promise toasts; latest-toast-wins with in-place `update`.
* `NotchToaster` with attached (notch) and floating modes at the top or
  bottom edge, and fluid, gooey, smooth, subtle and snappy motion presets.
* Inverted shadcn/ui neutral palette by default, customisable through
  `NotchToastStyle`.
* Wraps the camera cutout on notched phones, hiding the status bar while an
  attached top toast is visible.
* Live-region announcements, Escape to dismiss, and reduced-motion support.
